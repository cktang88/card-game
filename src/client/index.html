How to play
  • The battlefield is 5 columns. You hold a Front row and a Back row. Each round both players secretly place cards, then reveal together.
  • Place on any empty cell of your side (a Back cell needs your Front in that column). Drop a card on your own unit to stack it.
  • Units never move. Each strikes straight across its column: the enemy Front, or if that is empty, the enemy Back from behind (+1 rear strike). Ranged units in the Back fire over your line.
  • A Front unit with no enemy in its column is In Breach: instead of striking it flanks the enemy Front units beside it (+1 Shock to every hit they take, +2 if surrounded), and drains 1 enemy Resolve each round it holds.
  • Strikes deal Shock to Nerve; Lethal strikes wound Power. Morale heals, wounds don't: every round a unit regains 1 Nerve per ally around it (max 2). Isolated units crumble; tight formations hold.
  • At 0 Nerve a unit breaks: it routs back to your hand to be redeployed, its neighbours panic, and you lose 1 Resolve per card. At 0 Power it is killed for good. Reduce the enemy to 0 Resolve.
  • Ridge columns strike +1; Ruins columns blunt ranged fire. Escalate doubles the stakes; Retreat concedes.
