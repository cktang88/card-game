Break their will. Hold the line.

How to play
  • Each round both players secretly place cards, then reveal at the same time.
  • A new unit must touch your army (it grows out from your HQ). Placing it next to an enemy is how you attack. Drop a card on your own unit to stack it (Power and Nerve add up).
  • Units never move. After reveal, every unit strikes once, all at the same time: nearest enemy, then the one with least Nerve. Arrows show it before you lock in.
  • A strike deals Shock equal to its Power: +1 from higher ground, +1 for each other ally touching the target (max +2), −1 shooting into cover. Lethal units deal damage to Power instead.
  • At 0 Nerve a unit breaks and leaves; at 0 Power it is destroyed. Either way you lose 1 Resolve per card and its neighbours panic (1 Shock).
  • Whoever holds more Relays drains 1 enemy Resolve each round. Reduce the enemy to 0 Resolve, or have more after round 8.
  • Escalate doubles the stakes next round. Retreat concedes at the current stakes.
