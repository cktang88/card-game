How to play
  • The battlefield is 5 columns. You hold a Front row and a Back row. Each round both players secretly place cards, then reveal together.
  • Place on any empty cell of your side (a Back cell needs your Front in that column). Drop a card on your own unit to stack it.
  • Units never move. Each strikes straight across its column: the enemy Front, or the enemy Back if the Front is empty. Ranged units in the Back fire over your line.
  • Strikes deal Shock to Nerve; Lethal strikes wound Power.
  • At 0 Nerve a unit breaks: it routs back to your hand, the units around it panic (1 Shock), and you lose 1 Resolve per card. At 0 Power it is killed for good. Break the enemy army: reduce it to 0 Resolve.
  • Escalate doubles the stakes of the match; Retreat concedes at the current stakes.
