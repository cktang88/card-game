How to play
  • Each round both players secretly place cards, then reveal at the same time.
  • New units must touch your army (it grows out from your HQ). Drop a card onto your own unit to reinforce it.
  • Combat is automatic: units advance, then ranged fire, then melee clash. Arrows show what will happen.
  • Attacks deal Shock to Nerve. At 0 Nerve a unit breaks and its army loses Resolve. Flanks, high ground and surrounds win fights.
  • Hold Relays for Command and to drain enemy Resolve. Reduce the enemy to 0 Resolve to win.
  • Escalate doubles the stakes next round. Retreat concedes at the current stakes.
