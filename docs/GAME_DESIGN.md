# SHATTERLINE: Game Design (v0.4, the Battle Line)

> A casual-to-learn, obviously skill-based sci-fi card game about **breaking the enemy's
> will**. Two armies face each other across a five-column battle line. Both players place
> cards hidden and at the same time; everything then resolves on its own. **Gaps in a line
> are the whole game:** an empty column is a breach, a breach flanks the enemies beside it,
> and a unit flanked from both sides is surrounded.

- Research and the board debate behind this version: [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md) §11
- Card list with each card's niche: [`CARDS.md`](CARDS.md), generated by `npm run cards`
- Balance: `npm run sim -- 100` (faction level) and `npm run sim -- 20 --arch` (archetypes)

## 1. Pillars

| Pillar | Source of the idea | In the rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Every decision is a placement** | Snap, Mechabellum, Stormbound | You choose what, where, and how much to stake. Nothing else. |
| **Zero targeting ambiguity** | Inscryption's "attack the slot opposite" | Every unit has *one* arrow: straight across its column. |
| **Gaps, flanks and the rear decide morale** | Commands & Colors sections, Battle Line, Total War chain routs, DBA overlaps | Breach, then Flank, then Surround. Hits from behind are +1. Routs ripple to neighbours. |
| **Morale is the real health bar** | Infinity Wars (Health *or* Morale) | Strikes deal Shock to Nerve. Most losses are breaks, not kills. |
| **Simple** | The designer: "HQ is BS" | No HQ, no movement, no hexes, no line of sight, no facing, no relays. |
| **The gamble** | Snap | Escalate doubles the stakes; Retreat concedes. |

## 2. The complete rules

**Board**
- 5 columns. Each player has a **Front** row and a **Back** row (20 cells). A trench runs between the two Front rows.
- Two random columns get terrain:
  - **Ridge:** units there strike +1.
  - **Ruins:** ranged strikes *into* that column are −1.

**Setup**
- 12 Resolve, a 20-card deck, and a 5-card hand.

**Each round**
1. **Command:** gain `round + 1` (max 7). **Draw** 2.
2. **Orders (hidden, simultaneous):**
   - Place unit cards on any **empty cell of your side**. A Back cell needs your Front cell in that column filled, either already or by these same orders.
   - Or place onto **your own unit** to stack it: up to 3 cards (Swarm up to 5). Power and Nerve add up, and only the top card's text is active.
   - Play Tactics on a target.
   - Optionally Escalate.
3. **Reveal:** cards land. On Reveal effects and Tactics resolve.
4. **Breach:**
   - A Front unit with **no enemy anywhere in its column** is **In Breach**. It does not strike this round.
   - Each enemy Front unit in a **neighbouring column** of a breach is **Flanked**: +1 Shock on every strike it takes this round. Flanked from both sides means **Surrounded**: +2.
   - The board edge never counts as a breach.
5. **Strike:** every unit strikes once, all at the same time, at its **single target**:
   - **Front unit:** the enemy Front unit in its column. If that cell is empty, it hits the enemy Back unit **from behind (+1 rear strike)**.
   - **Ranged unit in the Back:** fires over its own line at the same target.
   - **Melee unit in the Back:** does not strike. It holds the column, so the enemy can't breach it.
   - **Strike value** = Power + bonuses (Ridge, rear, flank, card text). It deals that much **Shock** to Nerve. **Lethal** strikes deal damage to Power instead.
6. **Fallout:**
   - A unit at 0 Nerve **breaks** and leaves; a unit at 0 Power is destroyed.
   - Its owner loses **1 Resolve per card** in it.
   - The units **beside, in front of and behind it** panic: 1 Shock each. This cascades.
7. **Upkeep:**
   - "Each round" effects.
   - From round 2, if any of your units that were In Breach still stand, the enemy loses **1 Resolve** (max 1 per round).

**Winning**
- Reduce the enemy to 0 Resolve.
- Or, after round 8, have more Resolve. A tie goes to whoever holds more Front cells.

**Stakes**
- Escalate (once) doubles the stakes from the next round. Stakes also double for round 8.
- Retreat concedes at the current stakes.

**Fearless** units never break; Shock hits them as damage instead. This covers structures and Brood next to a Synapse.

## 3. Factions

| Faction | Rules | What it wants on the line |
|---|---|---|
| **Concord Vanguard** | **Shoulder to Shoulder:** a Front unit with an allied Front unit beside it strikes +1. Officers buff the units around them. | An unbroken line. It punishes gaps. |
| **Vhorr Brood** | **Flood:** a Brood unit In Breach flanks for +2. **Synapse:** Brood around a Synapse are Fearless. **Evolve:** +1 Power on striking (max +3). | Pour into gaps and surround. |
| **The Hush** | **Cloak:** ranged units can't target it, and their shots fall through to the unit behind. Being struck reveals it. **Ambush:** its first strike from cloak is +1. **Phantoms:** when struck they pop and Shock the striker. They hold a column for a round and cost no Resolve. | Fake gaps, fake walls, knives from nowhere. |

## 4. Keywords (column terms)

| Keyword | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lethal | Deals damage, not Shock |
| Hunter | Strikes the enemy Back row first |
| Guard | Strikes aimed at the allies beside it hit it instead |
| Artillery | Ignores Ruins and Guard |
| Armor | −1 damage taken |
| Raider | +2 on rear strikes |
| Cleave | Also strikes the enemies beside its target |
| Splash | 1 Shock to the enemies beside its target |
| Terror | +2 against units at half Nerve or less |
| Breach (Siege-Crawler) | +2 against Fearless units or units in Ruins |
| Fortify (Sappers) | Its column becomes Ruins |
| Shove (Ram-Track) | Moves the enemy Front unit opposite one column aside. It's the only mover |

## 5. Rule audit v0.3 → v0.4

**Cut:**
- The hex grid, craters and elevation levels.
- The HQ and touch-your-army placement.
- Nearest-enemy targeting and its tiebreaks.
- "+1 per ally touching the target" flanking.
- The same-hex contest.
- Relays and the relay majority drain.
- The v0.3 Cloak ("only adjacent enemies can hit it").

**Added (each one rule):**
- Front/Back rows.
- Breach, Flank and Surround.
- Rear strike.
- Breakthrough drain (replaces Relays).
- Column terrain (Ridge / Ruins).
