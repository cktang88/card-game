# SHATTERLINE: Game Design (v0.3, simplified)

> A casual-to-learn, obviously skill-based sci-fi card game on a hex board. Armies lose by
> **breaking**, not by being ground down. Every decision is a placement: both players
> place cards hidden and at the same time, then everything resolves on its own.
> **Units never move.**

- Research behind every rule: [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md)
- Full card list with each card's niche: [`CARDS.md`](CARDS.md), generated by `npm run cards`
- Balance numbers: `npm run sim -- 40` and `npm run sim -- 20 --arch`

---

## 1. Pillars

| Pillar | Source of the idea | In the rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Every decision is a placement** | Stormbound, Mechabellum, Marvel Snap; Faeria and Duelyst show where full control goes wrong | You pick what, where, and how much to stake. Nothing else. |
| **Simple to learn, obviously skill-based** | Snap's "low complexity, lots of depth" | About a dozen core rules. The skill is in reading your opponent and in board geometry. |
| **Deterministic and telegraphed** | Into the Breach; Artifact and Battlegrounds show the failure | One targeting rule, no dice, and intent arrows while you plan. |
| **Morale is the real health bar** | Infinity Wars (Health or Morale), 40k Battle-shock | Strikes deal Shock to Nerve. Breaking is the main way units leave the battle. |
| **Every rout has an author** | Red-team design test | Breaks come from flanks, high ground, panic chains, cut synapse links and decoys that someone placed. |
| **Obvious faction goals** | Infinity Wars and 40k | Concord holds the line, Brood swarms and evolves, the Hush strike unseen. |
| **Three numbers per card** | Gwent | Cost, Power, Nerve, plus a range icon. One line of text. |
| **The gamble** | Snap | Escalate doubles the stakes; Retreat concedes. |

## 2. The complete rules

**Setup**
- Board: 45 hexes with high ground (+1 or +2), ruins (cover), craters (no placement), and 3 Relays in the centre row.
- Each player has an HQ on their home edge. It anchors placement and cannot be attacked.
- Each player starts with 9 Resolve, a 20-card deck and a 5-card hand.

**Each round**
1. **Command:** gain `round + 1` Command (max 7). Unspent Command is lost.
2. **Draw** 2 cards.
3. **Orders (hidden, simultaneous):**
   - Place unit cards on empty hexes that **touch one of your units or your HQ**. A new unit may touch enemies; placing next to them is how you attack.
   - Or place a unit **onto your own unit** to stack it: up to 3 cards (Swarm up to 5). A stack's Power and Nerve add up, and only the top card's text is active.
   - Play Tactic cards on a target.
   - Optionally **Escalate**.
4. **Reveal:**
   - Cards land.
   - If both players chose the same hex, the player with initiative gets it and the other card returns to hand.
   - *On Reveal* effects resolve, then Tactics.
5. **Strike:** every unit strikes once, **all at the same time**.
   - **Target:** the nearest enemy in reach (melee means adjacent; ranged means within N hexes). Ties go to the enemy with less Nerve, then the leftmost.
   - **Strike value** = Power, then:
     - +1 if the attacker is on higher ground.
     - +1 for each other ally of the attacker touching the target (max +2).
     - −1 when shooting into cover.
     - Card bonuses.
   - The strike deals that much **Shock**, which lowers Nerve. **Lethal** strikes deal damage to Power instead.
6. **Fallout:**
   - A unit at **0 Nerve breaks**, and a unit at **0 Power is destroyed**. Either way it leaves the board.
   - Its owner loses **1 Resolve per card** in it. Tokens and decoys cost nothing.
   - Every adjacent ally **panics**: 1 Shock. Chains can cascade.
7. **Upkeep:**
   - "Each round" effects trigger.
   - Whoever holds **more Relays** drains 1 Resolve from the other.

**Winning**
- Reduce the enemy to 0 Resolve.
- Or, after round 8, have more Resolve. Ties go to the player holding more Relays.

**Stakes**
- Escalate (once per match) doubles the stakes from the next round. Stakes also double for round 8.
- Retreat concedes at the current stakes.

**Fearless** units never break; Shock hits them as damage instead. This covers structures and Brood linked to a Synapse.

## 3. Factions (one or two rules each)

| Faction | Rule | Goal you can read at a glance | Archetypes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Concord Vanguard** | **Shoulder to Shoulder:** a Concord unit next to 2+ allies strikes +1. | Hold a tight line | Gunline · Bastion · Iron Tide |
| **Vhorr Brood** | **Synapse Link:** Brood next to a Synapse are Fearless. **Evolve:** a Brood unit that strikes gains +1 Power (max +3). | Swarm, feed, grow | Endless Tide · Apex · Synaptic Terror |
| **The Hush** | **Cloak:** arrives cloaked, and only adjacent enemies can strike it. Placing next to it reveals it. **Ambush:** its first strike from cloak is +1. | Strike unseen | Phantom Host · Night of Knives · Dread |

## 4. Rule audit (v0.2 → v0.3): does each rule earn its keep?

**Kept:**

| Rule | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hidden simultaneous placement | **Kept** | Core skill: reading the opponent. |
| Touch-your-army placement (Hive) | **Kept, loosened** | Gives position and front-lines meaning. Touching enemies is now allowed, because that is how you attack. |
| Stacking | **Kept, loosened** | Concentrate or spread is a real decision. The same-class requirement was dropped. |
| One targeting rule | **Kept, simplified** | Nearest, then least Nerve, then leftmost. The front arc was dropped. |
| High ground +1, cover −1 vs ranged | **Kept** | Cheap, visible terrain decisions. |
| Flanking (+1 per other ally touching the target) | **Kept, simplified** | Surrounding is the most intuitive tactic. "Support −1" was dropped as its mirror. |
| Panic on loss | **Kept** | It's what makes the game about morale: cascades reward focus. |
| Fearless (one unified rule) | **Kept, unified** | Structures and linked Brood now follow the same rule. |
| Relays | **Kept, simplified** | A majority drains 1 (Snap's "win 2 of 3"). They no longer give Command. |
| Escalate / Retreat | **Kept** | The gamble layer costs zero board complexity. |

**Cut:**

| Rule | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Movement / advance step | **Cut** | Units stay where placed. Movement exists only on cards (Lancer Ram-Track's shove). |
| Flee, regroup, feral, vanish, surrender | **Cut** | A broken unit just leaves. Rallying is a card (Loudhorn Broadcast). |
| Shaken state (−1 when low) | **Cut** | Now only a "Wavering" warning label, with no rule attached. |
| Nerve recovery each round | **Cut** | Only card effects restore Nerve. Damage accumulates, so games are decisive. |
| Line of sight | **Cut** | Range is just distance. Cover still exists. |
| Separate ranged/melee steps | **Cut** | One simultaneous strike. |
| Front arcs / facing | **Cut** | Flanking is "allies touching the target". |
| Heavy units arriving a round late | **Cut** | Big cards simply cost more. |
| HQ siege, HQ as a target | **Cut** | The HQ is only a placement anchor. |
| Command carry-over, Command from Relays | **Cut** | Command = round + 1. |
| Class-matching for stacks | **Cut** | Any of your units can stack (structures and decoys can't). |
| Hush Vanish (broken units return to hand) | **Cut** | Worth about 25 win-rate points in simulation, and an extra rule. |

## 5. Card design rules

Taken from the TCG pattern research in RESEARCH.md §9:
- **One keyword or trigger, one line of text.** Vanilla cards get flavour text instead.
- Every card has a **niche**: the placement question it answers. It is recorded in `cards.ts` and shown in-game on hover.
- **About 10 global keywords:** Lethal, Hunter, Guard, Artillery, Armor, Fearless, Infiltrate, Cleave, Splash, Terror. Each faction adds 1–2 rules.
- **Every strong effect has a visible counter.**

  | Effect | Counter |
  |---|---|
  | Guard | Artillery |
  | Cloak | Placing adjacent |
  | Fearless | Lethal / Breach |
  | Synapse | False Orders, Hunter |
  | Clumping | Splash / Cleave / Nightmare |

- **Movement is a rare, premium exception** (one card).
- **Growth is capped:** Evolve +3, Grub +4.

## 6. Latest simulation (v0.3, greedy 1-ply AI)

- **Faction win rate (balanced decks, 120 games):** Concord 46%, Brood 50%, Hush 46%.
- **Archetype win rate (540 games):**

  | Faction | Archetypes |
  |---|---|
  | Concord | gunline 51, bastion 41, iron 46 |
  | Brood | tide 42, apex 48, synapse 62 |
  | Hush | phantom 47, knives 42, dread 56 |

- **Resolve lost by source:** morale 46%, kills 29%, relays 25%.
- **Game length:** most games end in rounds 6–8, and about 70% end by army collapse.
- **Known limits:**
  - The AI assumes the opponent plays nothing this round.
  - Synapse is slightly high; watch it.
